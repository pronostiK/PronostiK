The '''2025–26 Copa del Rey''' (branded as the '''Copa del Rey MAPFRE''' for sponsorship reasons) was the 124th staging of the [[Copa del Rey]] (including two seasons where two rival editions were played). The winners were assured a place in the [[2026–27 UEFA Europa League|2026–27 UEFA Europa League league stage]]. Both the winners and the runners-up would qualify for the four-team [[2027 Supercopa de España]].

[[FC Barcelona|Barcelona]] were the defending champions, having beaten [[Real Madrid CF|Real Madrid]] in the [[2025 Copa del Rey final|final of the previous edition]],<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.theguardian.com/football/2025/apr/27/barcelona-real-madrid-copa-del-rey-match-report|title=Barcelona win thrilling Copa del Rey and drive Madrid to red card fury|date=27 April 2025|work=The Guardian|access-date=1 May 2025}}</ref> but were eliminated by [[Atlético Madrid]] in the semi-finals.

The final was held at [[Estadio de La Cartuja|La Cartuja]] in [[Seville]] on 18 April 2026. [[Real Sociedad]] defeated Atlético Madrid 4–3 on penalties to win their third Copa del Rey title.

As across Spain, match times up to 26 October 2024 and from 30 March 2025 are [[Central European Summer Time|CEST]] ([[UTC+02:00|UTC+2]]). Times on interim ("winter") days are [[Central European Time|CET]] ([[UTC+01:00|UTC+1]]). Matches played in the Canary Islands use the [[Western European Time|WET]] ([[UTC±00:00]]).

==Schedule and format==
On 30 June 2025, the [[Royal Spanish Football Federation|RFEF]] introduced changes in the format of the competition, with the preliminary stage becoming a two-legged tie. The first and second rounds remained a single match, but a new criteria of geographical proximity was created to define which teams would face each other in these rounds.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.tribunasoria.com/noticias/410039/la-previa-de-la-copa-del-rey-se-jugara-a-ida-y-vuelta|title=La previa de la Copa del Rey se jugará a ida y vuelta|trans-title=The preliminary round of the Copa del Rey will be played in two legs|publisher=Tribuna Soria|language=es|date=30 June 2025|accessdate=11 July 2025}}</ref> In the round of 32 and in the round of 16, Supercopa de España teams were separated into a new pot, facing the teams from lowest divisions in both rounds.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.marca.com/futbol/2025/06/30/cambios-copa-rey-2025-2026-beneficiaran-equipos-supercopa.html|title=Los cambios en la Copa del Rey 2025/2026 que beneficiarán a los equipos de la Supercopa|trans-title=The changes in the 2025/2026 Copa del Rey which will benefit the Supercopa teams|publisher=[[Marca (newspaper)|Marca]]|language=es|date=30 June 2025|access-date=11 July 2025}}</ref>

==First round==
The first round was played by 112 of the 116 qualified teams, with the exceptions being the four participants of the [[2026 Supercopa de España]]. 

This first round remained a single match, but a new criteria of geographical proximity was created to define which teams would face each other. 

A total of 56 matches were played between 28 and 30 October 2025.

==Round of 32==
===Draw===
The draw for the round of 32 was held on 9 December 2025 in the RFEF headquarters in [[La Ciudad del Fútbol|Las Rozas]]. The four participant teams of the [[2026 Supercopa de España]] were drawn with the teams from the lowest category. The remaining teams from the lowest categories faced the rest of La Liga teams. Matches were played at stadiums of lower-ranked teams.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://rfef.es/es/noticias/9-de-diciembre-sorteo-de-los-dieciseisavos-de-final-de-la-copa-del-rey-mapfre|title=9 de diciembre, sorteo de los dieciseisavos de final de la Copa del Rey Mapfre|publisher=RFEF|language=es|access-date=27 November 2025}}</ref>

A total of 16 matches were played between 16 December 2025 and 6 January 2026. The match involving Rayo Vallecano was played during the week of 5 January due to a clash with the [[2025–26 UEFA Conference League league phase|UEFA Conference League league phase]] during the same week.

==Round of 16==

=== Draw ===
The draw for the round of 16 was held on 7 January 2026 in the RFEF headquarters in [[La Ciudad del Fútbol|Las Rozas]]. The four teams participating in the [[2026 Supercopa de España]] were drawn with the Segunda División teams. The remaining Segunda División team was drawn against a La Liga team. Matches were played at the stadiums of the lower-ranked teams, otherwise the first team drawn played at home. 

A total of eight matches were played between 13 and 15 January 2026.

A total of four matches were played between 3 and 5 February 2026.
