The '''2021–22 Copa del Rey''' was the 120th staging of the [[Copa del Rey]] (including two seasons where two rival editions were played). The winners were assured a place in the [[2022–23 UEFA Europa League#Group stage|2022–23 UEFA Europa League group stage]]. Both the winners and the runners-up qualified for the four-team [[2023 Supercopa de España]].

[[FC Barcelona|Barcelona]] were the defending champions, but they were eliminated by previous two seasons' runners-up [[Athletic Bilbao|Athletic Club]] in the round of 16. [[Real Betis]] won [[2022 Copa del Rey Final|the final]] 5–4 over [[Valencia CF|Valencia]] on [[Penalty shoot-out (association football)|penalties]] following a 1–1 draw after [[Overtime (sports)#Association football|extra time]] for their third Copa del Rey title.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/football/61202911|title=Real Betis win Copa del Rey after beating Valencia on penalties|website=BBC Sport|date=23 April 2022|access-date=23 April 2022}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.theguardian.com/football/2022/apr/24/real-betis-valencia-copa-del-rey-final-match-report |title=Joaquín joy as Real Betis beat Valencia on penalties to lift Copa del Rey|first=Sid|last=Lowe|work=The Guardian|date=23 April 2022|access-date=24 April 2022}}</ref>

As across Spain, match times up to 31 October 2021 and from 27 March 2022 were [[Central European Summer Time|CEST]] ([[UTC+02:00|UTC+2]]). Times on interim ("winter") days were [[Central European Time|CET]] ([[UTC+01:00|UTC+1]]). Matches played in the Canary Islands used the [[Western European Time|WET]] ([[UTC±00:00]]).

==First round==
The first round was played by 112 of the 116 qualified teams, with the exceptions being the four participants of the [[2022 Supercopa de España]]. The ten winners from the previous preliminary round were paired with ten teams from [[2021–22 La Liga|La Liga]]. The four [[2021 Copa Federación de España|Copa Federación]] semi-finalists with another four teams from [[2021–22 La Liga|La Liga]] and the last two [[2021–22 La Liga|La Liga]] teams with two teams from [[2021–22 Segunda División RFEF|Segunda RFEF]]. Twenty-one teams from [[2021–22 Segunda División RFEF|Segunda RFEF]] were paired with the twenty-one [[2021–22 Segunda División|Segunda División]] teams, the last nine teams from [[2021–22 Segunda División RFEF|Segunda RFEF]] were paired with nine [[2021–22 Primera División RFEF|Primera RFEF]] teams. Finally, the remaining twenty teams from [[2021–22 Primera División RFEF|Primera RFEF]] were paired between them. In the case of opponents from the same division, the home advantage was decided by whichever team was drawn first; otherwise, the match was held in the stadium of the lower division team. A total of 56 games were played from 30 November to 2 December 2021.

==Second round==
===Draw===
The draw was held on 3 December 2021 in the RFEF headquarters in [[La Ciudad del Fútbol|Las Rozas]]. Teams were divided into 4 pots according to their division in the 2021–22 season. Matches were played at the stadiums of lower-ranked teams. A total of 28 games were played from 14 to 16 December 2021.

==Round of 32==
===Draw===
The draw was held on 17 December 2021 in the RFEF headquarters in [[La Ciudad del Fútbol|Las Rozas]]. The four participant teams of the [[2022 Supercopa de España]] were drawn with the teams from the lowest category. After them, the remaining teams from the lowest categories faced the rest of La Liga teams. Matches were played at stadiums of lower-ranked teams.

==Round of 16==
===Draw===
The draw was held on 7 January 2022 in the RFEF headquarters in [[La Ciudad del Fútbol|Las Rozas]]. Teams are divided into 3 pots according to their division in the 2021–22 season. When possible, matches will be played at the stadiums of lower-ranked teams. Otherwise, the first team drawn plays home. A total of 8 games will be played from 15 to 20 January 2022.
