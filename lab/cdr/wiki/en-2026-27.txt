The '''2026–27 Copa del Rey''' is the 125th staging of the [[Copa del Rey]] (including two seasons where two rival editions were played). The winners are assured a place in the [[2027–28 UEFA Europa League#League Phase|2027–28 UEFA Europa League league phase]]. Both the winners and the runners-up will qualify for the four-team [[2028 Supercopa de España]].

[[Real Sociedad]] are the defending champions, having beaten [[Atlético Madrid]] in the [[2026 Copa del Rey final|final of the previous edition]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.espn.com/soccer/report/_/gameId/401859852|title=Real Sociedad upset Atlético Madrid to win 4th Copa del Rey|website=ESPN|date=18 April 2026|access-date=14 May 2026}}</ref>

The final will be held at [[Estadio de La Cartuja|La Cartuja]] in [[Seville]] in April 2027.

As across Spain, match times up to 25 October 2026 and from 29 March 2027 are [[Central European Summer Time|CEST]] ([[UTC+02:00|UTC+2]]). Times on interim ("winter") days are [[Central European Time|CET]] ([[UTC+01:00|UTC+1]]). Matches played in the Canary Islands use the [[Western European Time|WET]] ([[UTC±00:00]]).

==First round==
The first round will be played by 112 of the 116 qualified teams, with the exceptions being the four participants of the [[2027 Supercopa de España]]. 

This first round remains a single match, but a new criteria of geographical proximity was created to define which teams would face each other. 

A total of 56 matches will be played between 27 October and 18 November 2026. All matches scheduled to occur on 28 October were postponed due to a [[2026 Toledo train collision|train collision]] in [[Urda, Toledo]].<ref name=M2810>{{cite web|url=https://www.marca.com/futbol/copa-rey/2026/10/28/suspendida-jornada-copa-tragico-accidente-urda.html|title=Suspendida la jornada de Copa por el trágico accidente en Urda|trans-title=Cup round suspended due to the tragic accident in Urda|publisher=[[Marca (newspaper)|Marca]]|language=es|date=28 October 2026|accessdate=28 October 2026}}</ref> The matches originally scheduled for between 3 and 5 November were postponed once again as a mark of mourning for the passing of Tenerife player [[Jesús de Miguel]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://rfef.es/es/noticias/la-rfef-suspende-la-jornada-de-futbol-de-este-3-de-noviembre-tras-el-fallecimiento-de|title=La RFEF suspende la jornada de fútbol de este 3 de noviembre tras el fallecimiento de Jesús de Miguel, jugador del CD Tenerife|trans-title=The RFEF suspends this November 3rd's round of football matches following the death of CD Tenerife player Jesús de Miguel|publisher=[[Royal Spanish Football Federation]]|language=es|date=3 November 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://rfef.es/es/noticias/la-rfef-acuerda-ampliar-la-suspension-de-sus-competiciones-hasta-el-6-de-noviembre|title=La RFEF acuerda ampliar la suspensión de sus competiciones hasta el 6 de noviembre|trans-title=The RFEF agrees to extend the suspension of its competitions until 6 November|publisher=[[Royal Spanish Football Federation]]|language=es|date=3 November 2026}}</ref>
